Onera SleepScorer

Alexandru Lupu · github.com/alexandru-lupu-sci
  • Universal EEG/PSG Support: Reads BrainVision (.vhdr), EDF/EDF+ (.edf), BioSemi (.bdf), MNE FIF (.fif), EEGLAB (.set), and GDF (.gdf) via chunked streaming with single-session caching, automatically extracting .vmrk, embedded MNE annotations, or BIDS *_events.tsv markers.
  • Automated & Manual Sleep Staging: Automated mode scores 30-second epochs at 100 Hz with YASA's pre-trained LightGBM classifiers while keeping all display waveforms at the recording's native sampling rate; Manual mode bypasses YASA directly.
  • Two-Scorer Consensus Mode: Compares two scorers epoch-by-epoch, auto-validates union +MA tags, reports Agreement % & Cohen's Kappa, and optionally adds YASA as a 3rd opinion or confidence tie-breaker.
  • Wake Interval Overrides: Forces epochs before LightsOff and between matched Start/Stop ignore marker pairs to Wake (W).
  • Interactive Canvas Triage & Multi-Format Export: Zoom, pan, click, or box-select epochs directly on the hypnogram for crisp native-rate waveform inspection, stage / +MA edits, session resuming, and export to .Markers, .txt, BIDS *_events.tsv, MNE *_annotations.csv, Rich *_hypnogram.csv, or .vmrk.
